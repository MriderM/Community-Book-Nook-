Our Story

From Pop-Up to Community Hub

The Community Book Nook began as a small pop-up library in a local community centre in 2018. Founded by retired teacher, Eleanor Vance, it started with just a few shelves of donated books.

Its popularity grew as it became a gathering place for local book clubs, children's story hours, and community events. In 2021, it moved into a permanent, larger space to accommodate its expanding collection and programs, solidifying its role as a cornerstone of the community.

Our Mission

To foster a lifelong love of reading and learning by providing free and equitable access to books and educational resources for all members of our community.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, inclusive hub where the community connects, learns, and grows through the power of stories.

Meet Our Founder: Eleanor Vance

After 30 years of teaching, Eleanor retired with a vision to keep the joy of reading alive in her community. What started as a passion project with a handful of donated books has grown into a beloved community institution. Eleanor still volunteers weekly, reading to the children during Story Time.
